# Approvals

**Approval mode** is set by the publisher on the **slot** **terms**.

- **Required** — you must `request_approval`. The publisher sees it in Supply.
  Until they approve (or **allowlist** you), you cannot **buy** or open a CPC
  **campaign** on that inventory.
- **Waived** — you can buy or fund immediately with any active, non-**blocked**
  creative.

Requesting approval is a wallet transaction. It does not move USDC.
