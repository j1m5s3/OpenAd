# Approvals

An **approval** is your decision on one advertiser **creative**, reused across
all of your **slots**.

The Supply **approvals inbox** lists requests. Approve or reject each creative.

An **allowlist** is a blanket yes for an advertiser address: all of that
advertiser’s active creatives count as approved for you.

**Blocked** creatives (you rejected/revoked, or a **moderator** revoked globally)
are never served, even if **approval mode** is waived.
